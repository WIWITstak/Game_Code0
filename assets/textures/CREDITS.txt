Downloaded 2026-09-02. Used as equirectangular surface textures on the
in-game solar-system view. "_fictional" maps are artistic approximations.

Added 2026-09-06 (SSS, same CC BY 4.0):
- 2k_earth_specular_map.jpg — from SSS 2k_earth_specular_map.tif, greyscale ocean
  mask, converted to JPG. Drives the ocean sun-glint in Earth's day/night shader.
- 2k_venus_atmosphere.jpg  — SSS Venus cloud deck (replaces the radar surface
  map in the solar-system view).

Real elevation data (replaced the earlier fake "high-pass of albedo" maps),
added 2026-09-06:
- 2k_earth_normal_map.png — Sobel normal map computed from GEBCO_08 global
  elevation+bathymetry (NASA Visible Earth / eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov, image
  #73934, public domain; gebco_08_rev_elev_21600x10800.png).
- 2k_mercury_height.jpg — MESSENGER USGS Global DEM 665m
  (Mercury_Messenger_USGS_DEM_Global_665m_v2.tif), USGS Astrogeology PDS Annex,
  public domain. Decimated to 2048x1024, percentile-clipped, used as bumpMap.
- 2k_mars_height.jpg — MGS MOLA colour shaded relief, merged global 463m
  (NASA Trek layer Mars_MGS_MOLA_ClrShade_merge_global_463m, WMTS z4 8192x4096
  stitched then desaturated), public domain.
- 2k_moon_height.jpg — LRO LOLA global shaded relief 128ppd
  (Lunar_LRO_LOLA_Shade_Global_128ppd_v04.tif, USGS Astrogeology PDS Annex),
  public domain.
The Mars/Moon maps are hillshades of the real LOLA/MOLA terrain (a slight NW
lighting bias is baked in); Mercury is the raw DEM. All greyscale, used as
bumpMaps on the MeshStandardMaterial bodies; strength = debug panel "Relief".
